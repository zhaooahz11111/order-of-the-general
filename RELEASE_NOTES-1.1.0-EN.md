# Order of the General 1.1.0 — Free Playtest

![Stalingrad battlefield](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zhaooahz11111/order-of-the-general/main/screenshots/01-hero.png)

A free playtest of a card-driven hex-and-counter wargame set in Stalingrad, 1942.

Companies and battalions, 216 metres per hex, about one hour per round. You command through a hand of orders and a limited pool of command points — not through a perfect plan.

## Download

- `Order.of.the.General.English.Edition.Setup.1.1.0.exe` — English Edition installer

Windows 10/11 x64. Unsigned build; if SmartScreen appears, choose **More info → Run anyway**. Check `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## A card-driven wargame, computed by the machine

This is a wargame first: hexes, ZOC, line of fire, stacking, supply, morale, fieldworks and combined arms. It is card-driven second: orders are a hand of cards, not a menu. The computer resolves line of fire, range, terrain, stacking, supply, casualties, vehicles, morale, artillery sheaves and fieldworks — faster and more consistently than resolving the same detail by hand.

![AI vs human](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zhaooahz11111/order-of-the-general/main/screenshots/02-ai.png)

## A complete AI, not a scripted opponent

The AI uses the same decks, cards, command points and combat rules. It does not read your hand or hidden orders. It builds legal actions and evaluates objectives, terrain, stacking, supply, ZOC, fieldworks and threat; it prepares artillery, suppresses positions, uses observed fire, combines arms before an assault, and focuses fire on guns and broken remnants.

- **Easy** — simpler scoring, fewer CP
- **Normal** — full rules scoring
- **Hard** — wider candidates and one-ply lookahead
- **Extreme** — widest candidates and lookahead; artillery and combined-arms preparation before the assault

## Four ways to play

- **Solo** — one player controls both sides
- **Vs AI** — choose side and one of four difficulties; AI hand hidden
- **Hot-seat** — two players, one machine, hand-over mask
- **LAN PVP** — create or join a local-network room; server-authoritative, own hand only

LAN PVP is local-network play, not internet matchmaking. No account or online service required.

## What is in the playtest

- Four Stalingrad boards
- Company/battalion scale, 216 m hexes, about one hour per round
- Three decks: movement, attack and special; 4 normal + 3 special cards per phase; specials carry into the attack phase
- ZOC, infiltration move, artillery fire missions, fieldworks and encirclement
- Complete AI with four difficulty levels; solo, hot-seat and LAN PVP
- Save/load, in-game rules and tutorial; Chinese and English UI

> This is a free playtest build. Rules, card counts, balance and content may still change. Source code is not included.
