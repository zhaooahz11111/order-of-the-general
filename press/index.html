The game

Two campaigns. One ruleset.

Company and battalion scale, 216 metres per hex. The player commands through a hand of movement, attack and special cards; the computer resolves line of fire, ZOC, stacking, supply, morale, fieldworks, artillery sheaves and the opponent's legal actions. The AI uses the same decks, cards and combat rules as the player.

Why it plays differently

Design proof

  • 01
    The cards are the command layer.Four normal cards plus three specials each phase; movement and attack are separate physical decks. The player has the plan but not the tempo to do everything.
  • 02
    Combat is weapon-by-weapon.Every weapon and vehicle in the counter's kit contributes: rifles, machine guns, mortars, anti-tank guns, tank guns, flamethrowers. Range, penetration, cover, stance, fieldworks, morale and suppression all matter.
  • 03
    The AI plays the same game.Same decks, same cards, same CP, same combat rules. It prepares artillery, suppresses positions, combines arms and handles armour without reading the player's hand.
  • 04
    The English tutorial teaches the systems.Mouse controls, the deck split, attack preview, target highlighting, save/new battle, both hand-over screens and the combat model — not just a button-by-button walkthrough.
