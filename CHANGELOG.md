# Changelog

## 1.1.2 — 2026-10-26

Free playtest build.

### Deck system
- Split each side into three decks: movement, attack and special.
- 4 normal + 3 special cards per phase; specials carry into the attack phase.
- Random deck shuffles on every new game; dead all-normal hands redraw.
- At least one unit-reinforcement special is guaranteed.

### Rules
- ZOC-to-ZOC movement restriction and infiltration move card.
- Encirclement cuts supply.
- HQ/artillery screened by other friendly units in the same hex.
- Artillery fire missions can damage fieldworks (25% per covered hex).
- Retreat morale recovery increased to +20%.
- Airdrop, flak umbrella and national morale cards rebalanced.

### UI / presentation
- Shellfire impact markers clear after resolution.
- Full-range artillery fire-mission centres.
- Reconnaissance in place.
- Free advance after enemy retreat.
- Demolition clears fieldworks and shows an impact marker.
- CP descriptions follow actual difficulty/HQ values.
